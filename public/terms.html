  • Reports from players, which you can file on anyone from inside the game.
  • Review by the Game Master and our moderators, who can remove any post, mute an account, or ban it.

Reporting someone

If a player is making the city worse, report them rather than retaliating — retaliating usually just gets both of you actioned. A report records who you reported, where it happened, and what kind of behaviour it was. Because voice is never recorded, a voice report also records who else was near enough to hear, so a moderator has someone to ask. That means your name can appear as a witness on someone else's report if you were standing nearby.

Report honestly. Filing reports you know to be false, or organising others to mass-report someone, is itself a breach of these terms.

7. Voice chat

Voice is live and proximity-based: players near you in the city can hear you, and party voice reaches your party wherever they are. We do not record or store voice — it is relayed and discarded.

Because it is not recorded, we usually cannot verify what was said in a voice complaint. Treat voice as public speech in a public place: assume anyone nearby can hear you, and behave accordingly. Your microphone is only used when you choose to enable it.

9. Card games and chips: no gambling

LepakMamak does not offer gambling, wagering or real-money gaming of any kind.

The card and table games in the city, including poker, are played entirely for fun. To be completely clear about how they work:

  • Poker chips are free. Every player is given a fresh set of chips at the start of each hand.
  • Chips are completely separate from Syiling Lepak. Coins cannot be converted into chips, and chips cannot be converted into coins. There is no buy-in, and no purchase of any kind affects a game's outcome.
  • Chips have no value, do not carry over, cannot be transferred, and cannot be cashed out.
  • Nothing you can buy gives you an advantage in any game. Purchases are cosmetic only.

No stake, no prize, no payout — the games are entertainment, in the same way a board game at the table is entertainment. Attempting to use LepakMamak's games for real-money betting, staking, or any arrangement where anything of value changes hands based on an outcome is a serious breach of these terms and will result in permanent termination.
