1. Who we are

LepakMamak is a free, browser-based multiplayer game set in a miniature Kuala Lumpur, available at lepakmamak.my. In this policy, "we", "us" and "LepakMamak" mean the operator of that service. "You" means anyone who creates an account or plays the game.

We are the data controller for the personal data described here, and we handle it in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) and its amendments.

2. What we collect

We only collect what the game actually needs to work. Here is the full list.

Account details

You can sign in in two ways. If you create an account with an email address and a password, your password is hashed by our authentication provider — we never see it or store it in readable form. If you continue with Google, Google confirms your identity to us and shares your email address with us; we never receive your Google password.

Either way, you also choose a display name and a look for your character (colours and character style).

Your profile

You can optionally fill in profile details that other players can see: a short bio, your hometown, interests, languages you speak, social media handles, your favourite hangout, and your geng. All of this is voluntary — leave any of it blank and the game still works.

Chat and things you post

WhatDo we store it?
Public room chatYes. Saved with your display name so that recent conversation loads when someone walks into the room.
Party chat and direct messagesNo. These are delivered live to the people you sent them to and are never written to our database.
Voice chatNo. See what we don't collect.
Lepak Wall postsYes. Your text, plus any photo or voice note you attach.
Likes, replies, guestbook messagesYes. Stored with your display name.
Drawings in the drawing gameNo. These exist only for the length of the round and are then gone.

Gameplay data

We store your achievements, your social stats, a record of activity shown on your profile, and your Lepak Coin balance and its transaction history. We also keep a weekly tally of a couple of stats — tables sat at and basketball points — which appears with your display name on the public weekly leaderboard.

Reports and enforcement

When someone files a report about a player, we store who filed it and their display name, who it was filed against and their display name, the room, what kind of behaviour it was about, and any note the reporter wrote.

A report about voice also records which other players were close enough to hear at that moment. Because we never record audio, those witnesses are the only way a voice report can be reviewed fairly — so if you were near a conversation that gets reported, your display name may appear in that report as someone who may have heard it.

If we mute or ban an account, we store that decision, its reason, when it expires, and who made it.

Purchases

When you buy coins, payment is handled entirely by Stripe on Stripe's own hosted checkout page. Your card details never touch our servers — we never see or store your card number. What we keep on our side is a record of the purchase: the item, the amount, the Stripe reference for the transaction, and the resulting change to your coin balance. Your email address is passed to Stripe so it can send you a receipt.

Photos you upload

Photos posted to the Lepak Wall are automatically checked for prohibited content before the post goes live. To do this, the image is sent to OpenAI's moderation service. Voice notes are not scanned this way.

3. What we don't collect

This part matters as much as the rest, so we want to be specific:

  • We never record your voice. Voice chat is relayed live through our server to the players near you and is discarded immediately. Nothing is written to disk, nothing is stored, nothing can be played back later — not by us, not by anyone.
  • There is no advertising and no third-party tracking. We do not run Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any third-party tracking script. We do not build a profile of you for advertising, and we never sell your personal data.
  • We do not store your card details — Stripe handles payments end to end.
  • We do not store or log your IP address, and we do not fingerprint your device. Our server does read your IP address in memory, to count how many requests are coming from one place and stop a single source flooding the city. Those counters are thrown away every few seconds and are never written to a database. Separately, the infrastructure companies listed in section 6 see your IP address at their network edge, as any web host does, in order to route traffic and block abuse.
  • We count a few first steps, not how you play. To learn where new visitors give up, our own server counts seven moments: the page opening, tapping play, seeing the sign-up form, creating an account, entering the city, and the first time on a visit that you sit down and that you start a game. Each count carries a random number your browser makes up for itself — not your name, email or IP address, and nothing about your device — and your account id if you are signed in. It goes to our own database and to nobody else. We do not record where you walk, what you say, who you talk to or what happens inside a game. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, none of this is counted and the random number is never created. Our server measures its own load — bandwidth, voice packets, dropped frames — to know when it is running out of capacity. Those are totals for the whole server, not a record of you.
  • We do not ask for your real name, phone number, address, or date of birth.

4. Why we use it

  • To run the game — signing you in, placing your character in the city, delivering chat and voice to the people around you, and keeping your coins and items.
  • To make the social features work — showing your display name and profile to other players, and delivering Wall posts, likes and replies.
  • To keep the city safe — filtering offensive language in chat, screening uploaded photos, and dealing with content or behaviour that breaks our Terms of Service.
  • To process your purchases and give you what you paid for.
  • To fix problems and keep the service stable and secure.

Our lawful basis is your consent (given when you create an account), our need to perform the service you asked for, and our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe and working.

5. What other players can see

LepakMamak is a social game, so some information is visible to others by design:

  • Your display name, character appearance, and where you are in the city.
  • Anything you put in your public profile.
  • Your messages in public room chat, and your Lepak Wall posts, likes and replies.
  • Your achievements and profile activity.
  • Your display name and score on the weekly leaderboard, if you place on it.

Please read this before uploading a photo or voice note. Media you attach to a Lepak Wall post is stored in public cloud storage and served from a public web address. That means anyone who has the link can open the file directly, even if they are not signed in to LepakMamak. Deleting your post removes the file from our storage, but we cannot recall copies that someone has already downloaded or shared elsewhere. Do not post anything you would not be comfortable seeing outside the game.

Your email address, your password, and your purchase history are never shown to other players.

7. Cookies and local storage

LepakMamak does not set advertising or tracking cookies. We do not use a cookie banner because we do not place the kind of cookies that require one.

The game does save a few things in your browser's local storage so that it behaves the way you left it:

  • Your sign-in session, so you are not asked to log in on every visit.
  • Your settings — music and sound preferences, graphics quality, chat panel state, and your wardrobe choices.
  • Where you were standing in the city, and which places you have visited.
  • Your saved city progress.
  • The name you were given if you play as a guest, so you are the same guest next time.
  • A random visitor number, used only for the first-step counts described in section 3. It is not created if your browser asks not to be tracked.

This information stays in your browser. Clearing your browser data removes it, and doing so will sign you out and reset those preferences.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account details, profile, coins and items — kept while your account exists.
  • Public chat messages — currently kept indefinitely so that room history is available. We are working towards a fixed retention window; until then, treat public chat as a lasting record.
  • Wall posts and their media — kept until you or a moderator deletes them.
  • Purchase records — kept for as long as we need them for accounting, tax and dispute-handling purposes.
  • Moderation records — when a moderator removes a post, we keep a record of that action, including the content that was removed, so that decisions can be reviewed and appealed. This means removed content can survive in our moderation log after it has disappeared from the game.
  • Reports — kept while under review and afterwards as a record of the decision, so repeat behaviour can be recognised and an appeal can be checked.
  • Mutes and bans — the record of a live penalty is deleted when that penalty is lifted or expires.
  • First-step counts — deleted after thirteen months. If you delete your account, your account id is removed from them straight away.
  • Voice — not kept at all.

9. How we protect it

  • All traffic between you and LepakMamak is encrypted over HTTPS and secure WebSockets.
  • Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Our database is locked down with row-level security. Public and browser-side keys cannot read other players' private data; only our game server, using a privileged key held on the server, can.
  • Our moderation console sits behind a separate identity check and is restricted to a single authorised administrator account.
  • Card payments are handled entirely by Stripe, so card data never enters our systems.

No online service can promise perfect security, but if a breach ever affects your personal data we will act on it and notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under the PDPA you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Withdraw your consent to our processing, and to limit how we use your data.
  • Ask us to delete your account and personal data.
  • Complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Malaysia.

Some of this you can do yourself inside the game: edit your profile at any time, delete your own Wall posts, and remove guestbook entries on your profile.

Account deletion. You can delete your account yourself from the security settings inside the game. Doing so removes your Wall posts and their media, your replies and likes, guestbook entries you wrote or received, your profile activity, achievements and stats, your items, and your chat messages — and then the account itself. It cannot be undone.

A few things survive, and you should know which. Purchase records are kept because we need them for accounting and tax. Moderation records are kept so that decisions stay reviewable — this includes content a moderator removed for breaking the rules, and any report filed about you or by you, which keeps the display name recorded at the time but no longer links to your account.

If you would rather we did it for you, email hello@lepakmamak.my from the address on your account.

We will respond to any request within the period required by law, and we will not charge you for a reasonable request.

11. Children and young players

LepakMamak is not intended for children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to create an account. If you are under 18, you may only use LepakMamak with the consent of a parent or guardian, and you must have their permission before making any purchase.

The game includes live text and voice chat with other players, which parents should be aware of before allowing a young person to play.

If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us at hello@lepakmamak.my and we will remove the account and its data.

12. Where your data is stored

The service providers listed in section 6 operate data centres in a number of countries, and some of them are outside Malaysia. This means your personal data may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in another country. We choose established providers that offer protections consistent with the PDPA's requirements for cross-border transfers, and we only send them what they need to perform their function.
