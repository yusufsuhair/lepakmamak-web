# LepakMamak: social growth roadmap

Positioning: **Jom mamak. Send link je.**

The first loop: invite a friend to your table → sit together → belanja teh tarik → share a funny Resit Lepak → friends join the same room and table.

## First release

- **Meja Kita:** named tables, live seat occupancy, a host while seated, same-room invitation links and arrival near the selected table. Existing exclusive chairs remain authoritative.
- **Boss, belanja satu:** free table rounds, visible cups and a shared cheers moment. No real payment or wallet currency.
- **Resit Lepak:** optional downloadable portrait receipt with the player's own session duration, drinks bought for friends, drinks received, recalls, table name and a join link. Never include recorded voice, chat quotes, or other players' names automatically.
- Guest entry stays available. Receipt counters describe the current connection/session; they are not permanent achievements. Table names last while the room exists. No account database migration in this release.

## Ideas to explore next

1. **Meja Kita, expanded:** friend lists, rejoin friends and persistent group tables.
2. **Drink expression:** fancy cups and serving animations; core social interaction stays free.
3. **Resit Lepak, expanded:** optional statistics, group photo framing and more receipt jokes.
4. **Hujan lah:** short rain gatherings, shared umbrellas, a dramatic Myvi arrival.
5. **Teh Tarik Tower:** a one-button cooperative pouring challenge; failed timing gives foam moustaches.
6. **Myvi Muat Tak:** parking challenges with friends giving directions and spectators scoring.
7. **Photobooth:** album covers at the mamak, Raya friend portraits, Myvi poses and recall photobombs.
8. **Pasar Malam:** scheduled hosted gatherings, temporary stalls and rotating activities.
9. **Geng:** group names, matching shirts and a shared photo wall.
10. **Mic Mamak:** hosted quizzes, stories and comedy with opt-in audience, speaking queue, mute/block/report.
11. **Benda pelik malam ni:** rotating playful surprises: helmet day, glowing freezer, a cat manager, giant inflatable teh tarik.
12. **Regular dekat sini:** optional earned social titles such as Warga Mamak and Tukang Belanja; no mandatory jobs or money HUD.

## Launch and business

Start with Meja Kita, drinks and receipts. Invite a small group of Malaysian creators to a hosted Malam Lepak. Concentrate people in existing rooms before adding locations. Sell outfits, group shirts, cup effects, poses and table decorations after people form groups; keep guest entry and basic shared activities free.

Measure guest-to-first-social-interaction time, invitation joins, friends returning together and receipt shares. These are hypotheses, not guarantees of virality. Keep mobile performance and nearby voice privacy as release requirements. Avoid adding server replicas before implementing room ownership/routing.

Research context: Roblox's published search/style review supports the importance of social play and self-expression, but does not validate these specific concepts: https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/12/roblox-replay-decoded-search-style
